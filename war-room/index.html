What their comp wants to do

Everything on their profiles points one direction: Anthonyza's Master Yi (2.8M pts) with Paeter's Zilean (1M pts) — speed, stun-shrugging ult timing, and a revive on a Diamond hypercarry — while Mehlo's dash assassin (Irelia/Qiyana/Kat) takes over mid. Their win condition is 20+ minute teamfights where Yi cleans up. Their weaknesses: the jungler owns exactly two champs, the top laner is shallow, and the whole plan folds to layered hard CC. Worth saying out loud: Master Yi and Zilean have essentially zero pro presence — this is a solo-queue comp, and it loses to the coordinated, CC-layered drafts pro teams run, which is exactly what your pools can build.

Ban strategy (tournament draft)

Phase 1 — break the Yi engine

  1. Master Yi — 2.8M pts on a Diamond II. Non-negotiable in game 1.
  2. Zilean — the enabler. Banning both Yi and Zilean deletes their comp's identity.
  3. Irelia or Qiyana — whichever your mid respects more; or skip this ban and pick Vex + tanks into Mehlo instead.

Phase 2 — react to their picks

  1. Jarvan IV (2.2M, and 48% pro priority) — if Yi is gone and Anthonyza hasn't picked yet.
  2. Katarina / Akali — if Mehlo still hasn't picked and both dashes remain open.
  3. Twisted Fate (997K) — if Paeter looks like he's going carry instead of support.

What they'll ban against you

  • Nidalee (Hamwow 953K, top-400 NA) — near lock.
  • Swain (Austin 888K) — likely.
  • Thresh / Briar / Xerath / Sejuani — the rest of the visible power. Every comp below has a backup at each slot.
